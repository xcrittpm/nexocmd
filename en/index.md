**NexoCMD** is a remote connection client for **Mac, iPhone and iPad**, built for system administrators, developers
and IT technicians: your servers, an SSH terminal, SFTP file transfer and, soon, RDP remote desktop, in one app.

- Full-window **SSH terminal** with tabs and themes.
- **SFTP file panel** next to the terminal that **follows your `cd`**.
- **Server status** at a glance: CPU, memory, disk, load, network and uptime.
- **Private by design**: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Passwords and keys stay in your device's Keychain.
