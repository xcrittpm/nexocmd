## Data we collect

**None.** NexoCMD does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal or usage data. It contains no
analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party tools that do so. No account is required.

## Data stored on your device

- **Connections and settings** (names, hosts, usernames, folders, tags, notes, trusted hosts): in the app's storage
  on your device.
- **Passwords, private keys and passphrases**: in your device's Keychain, encrypted by the system. They are never
  included in exports.

This data never leaves your device unless you export it or turn on iCloud sync.

## iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn it on in Settings, your connections, folders and trusted hosts are stored in the **private database of
your own iCloud account**, so you have them on your Mac, iPhone and iPad. They are **end-to-end encrypted**: only
your devices can read them; neither Apple nor we have access. We run no servers and never receive this data.

Passwords, private keys and passphrases are **not synced** unless you also choose to keep them in **iCloud
Keychain**, which Apple end-to-end encrypts and syncs across your devices.

Folders you mark as "only on this device" are not synced. You can turn sync off at any time, and delete NexoCMD's
data from iCloud in your device's iCloud settings.

## Clipboard

In Remote Desktop sessions, if "Share clipboard" is on, the text you copy is shared between your device and the
remote computer you are connected to. It never goes through any server of ours.

## Permissions

- **Files**: only the ones you choose to upload or import (for example, a Remote Desktop Manager export), and the
  Downloads folder on Mac to save downloads, screenshots and terminal text.
- **Photos** (iPhone and iPad): only to **add** a screenshot when you choose to save it there. The app never reads
  your photo library.
- **iCloud**: only if you turn on sync.
- **Network**: to connect to your servers.

## Network connections

The app only connects to the servers you configure, for the features you use. Traffic goes directly and encrypted
from your device to your server, never through any server of ours. The status bar runs **read-only** commands on
your server to display CPU, memory, disk and network usage; this information is shown on screen only.

## Children

NexoCMD is a professional tool and is not directed at children.
